
# ⛏️ MineGuard – AI-Powered Coal Mine Safety System

MineGuard is a coal mine safety project designed to improve worker safety by supporting the verification of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) before workers enter mining areas.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Coal mining environments involve serious safety risks. Workers entering mining areas without appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) may be exposed to hazardous conditions. Traditional manual checks can also be time-consuming and difficult to monitor consistently.

MineGuard explores a technology-driven approach to coal mine safety verification.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

MineGuard focuses on a digital safety-monitoring interface designed around a PPE verification workflow.

The main objectives are:
- Supporting PPE checks before mine entry.
- Providing a dashboard interface for safety-related information.
- Making safety verification easier to monitor and manage.
- Exploring AI-assisted technology for mining safety.

## ✨ Key Features and Highlights

- **Coal Mine Safety:** Focuses on worker safety during mine entry.
